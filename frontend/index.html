How it works

Three small habits. One steadier you.

  1. 01

    Check in

    Pick a face, add what's behind it. Ten seconds, no judgement. MindCare quietly learns your rhythm.

  2. 02

    Try something small

    Get a breathing, movement, mindfulness or reframing exercise matched to how you're actually feeling today.

  3. 03

    See what helps

    Watch your patterns surface — triggers, best times of day, what lifts you — and bring a real person in when it's time.
