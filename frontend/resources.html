Professional support

When and how to seek professional mental health support.

When to seek professional help

Persistent symptomsWhen symptoms interfere with daily life for more than two weeks.
Thoughts of self-harmAny thoughts of hurting yourself or others require immediate attention.
Relationship problemsSignificant issues with family, friends, or work relationships.
Substance useUsing alcohol or drugs to cope with emotional problems.

Types of professionals

PsychologistsProvide therapy and psychological testing. Hold doctoral degrees (PhD, PsyD).
PsychiatristsMedical doctors who can prescribe medication and provide therapy.
Licensed counselorsProvide therapy and counseling services. Hold master's degrees (LMFT, LCSW, LPC).
Social workersProvide therapy and connect clients with community resources (LCSW).
